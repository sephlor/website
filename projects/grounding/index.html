🪁 Grounding

Work in progress · pre-v1

A wind-forecast tool for kitesurfing spots that grounds an LLM's answers in real data instead of guesses. It resolves the folk names kiters actually use, like "3rd Ave" or "the Racetrack", to the launch-accurate coordinates where the forecast actually matters.

The interesting part is the spot-resolution layer: free-text names in, canonical spots out, with their coordinates, aliases, and region. Today it runs as a small MCP server pulling live Open-Meteo forecasts for a handful of Bay Area spots. The standalone resolution API and curated gazetteer are still being built.

Coming soon: instructions to try it out yourself.
